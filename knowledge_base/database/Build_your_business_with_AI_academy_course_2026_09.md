# Crea tu negocio con IA (Build Your Business with AI) - Academy Course

## Official Naming

- **Official title (Spanish)**: Crea tu negocio con IA
- **English title**: Build Your Business with AI
- **Also referred to informally as**: "Launch Your Business with AI", "Business with AI course"
- **Program type**: Academy course (asynchronous, video-based, self-paced short course) - NOT a bootcamp
- **Course language**: Spanish
- **Market focus**: Spain (use cases, legal/fiscal content, and market examples are contextualized for the Spanish market)

## Course Overview

This intensive course guides students through the complete process of creating and launching a digital business using **Artificial Intelligence** and **No-Code** tools. The approach is highly practical: students don't just learn concepts, they build a real project from scratch.

The course bridges the gap between having an idea and executing it. Traditionally, launching a digital business required programming, design, marketing, and operations knowledge. This course removes those barriers by teaching AI and No-Code tools that allow students to:

- **Validate ideas in days**, not months
- **Build functional MVPs** without writing code
- **Automate operations** from day one
- **Scale with AI agents** that work 24/7

### Format and Pedagogy

- Video-based, self-paced asynchronous learning (Academy format)
- Learning-by-doing methodology: each module combines conceptual lessons, hands-on tool tutorials, use cases contextualized for the Spanish market, quizzes to verify knowledge, and practical challenges
- Quizzes are required to progress; challenges are optional open-ended practice activities
- Students finish the course with their own project: a validated MVP, configured automations, and a growth plan

### Target Personas

Designed for profiles who want to create a digital business quickly and efficiently:

- **Professional with a side business**: works full-time, wants additional income (500-2000 EUR/month) without leaving their job
- **Serial entrepreneur**: enjoys launching projects quickly; wants to launch 3-5 products/year and see which gets traction
- **Professional in transition**: seeks autonomy; wants to create a business to eventually leave corporate work
- **Consultant/Freelancer**: wants to productize their knowledge - move from selling time to selling digital products
- **Student/Recent graduate**: wants to stand out; create a first business or an impressive portfolio

### Prerequisites

- **No programming or technical knowledge required**
- Basic computer and Internet skills
- Basic English (many tools are in English)
- Entrepreneurial mindset and willingness to experiment

## Learning Objectives

Upon completing this course, students will be able to:

1. **Identify and validate** real business opportunities using Lean methodologies and AI tools
2. **Design and build** a functional MVP using No-Code platforms like Lovable, Replit, and Bolt
3. **Implement digital marketing strategies** to acquire and retain users
4. **Automate processes** using tools like n8n and Make
5. **Create AI agents** that execute tasks and improve user experience
6. **Analyze key metrics** and adjust pricing strategies to optimize revenue
7. **Present projects** effectively to investors, partners, or potential clients
8. **Understand the fundamentals** of cloud infrastructure, databases, and application deployment
9. **Create visual identity** and marketing content with AI assistance
10. **Establish the appropriate legal and fiscal structure** for a digital business in Spain

## Module 1: Opportunity Identification & Validation

The most extensive module; establishes the foundations of the program. Students learn to transform vague ideas into structured, validated business opportunities using Lean methodologies and AI.

**Thematic units:**
- **Opportunities and Market with AI**: fundamentals of opportunity and pain points, identifying real opportunities, niche detection with AI, trend analysis, idea filtering. Tutorials: Perplexity, Google Trends, Exploding Topics, Product Hunt, Freemium
- **Idea Structuring**: Problem -> Solution -> Customer flow, Job to be Done (JTBD) concept, defining key components with AI assistance. Tutorials: Perplexity, Notion
- **Value Proposition and BMC**: Value Proposition Canvas, Business Model Canvas (simplified version)
- **Rapid Validation and Landing Pages**: Lean validation fundamentals, building a validation landing page, collecting and analyzing feedback with AI
- **Business Sheet and Presentation**: validated business sheet, elevator pitch, one-pager

## Module 2: MVP Construction with No-Code & AI

Students design and build a functional Minimum Viable Product (MVP) using No-Code and AI tools. Emphasis: the MVP's goal is to learn, not to build a perfect product.

**Thematic units:**
- **No-Code Development with AI**: building without programming, functional classification and priority tool stack
- **MVP Design**: MVP definition, feature prioritization framework, user journey design, Figma tutorial
- **Data Structure and Databases**: data structures, data types, relational and NoSQL database engines, document databases (MongoDB). Tutorials: Figma II, Eraser, Supabase, Airtable, Supabase MCP, MongoDB, MongoDB MCP
- **Visual Design and UX/UI**: MongoDB MCP and Neo4j tutorials, visual design and navigation (user experience)
- **AI Integration and Development**: feature prioritization, Neo4j MCP, Figma III, Eraser II, integrating AI into the MVP
- **Cloud Deployment and Containers**: AI image creation tools, AI integration and intelligent content, Lovable (building MVP screens and flow), Replit Agents, Bolt (automatic page generation), solution deployment, cloud computing

## Module 3: How to Promote and Grow Your Business

Students implement effective digital marketing strategies to acquire their first users, measure results, and optimize MVP growth.

**Thematic units:**
- **Digital Marketing and Metrics**: marketing funnel, fundamental growth metrics, initial acquisition channels and strategies, product marketing vs content marketing
- **Branding and Content with AI**: Lean marketing (Test, Measure, Iterate), visual branding fundamentals with AI, textual branding and copywriting with AI, AI content creation for social media
- **Advertising Campaigns**: acquisition channels, Freepik and Canva tutorials, rapid test funnel fundamentals

## Module 4: Automation & AI Agents

Students automate operational and business processes and create AI agents that execute tasks without human intervention - transforming businesses from "high manual effort" to "automated operations".

**Thematic units:**
- **Business Automation**: introduction to business automation, classifying automatable processes, key tools (n8n, Make, etc.)
- **Automation Flows with n8n**: No-Code/AI automation tools, quick start, first flow from scratch
- **AI Agents Fundamentals**: flows with Gmail trigger and AI, complete use case, what an AI agent is and its applications
- **Building AI Agents**: agent training and configuration, AI agent best practices, RAG database creation in n8n, agent creation in n8n (production RAG agent challenge)
- **Prompting and LLM Security**: Copilot Studio tutorial, Replit Agent tutorial, security with LLMs

## Module 5: Metrics, Pricing & Monetization

Students learn to measure business performance through key metrics, define effective pricing strategies, optimize for sustainable growth, and establish the legal structure of the business.

**Thematic units:**
- **Business Metrics**: metrics and decision making, practical measurement without technicalities, real examples in Spain (Factorial, Glovo, Wallapop). Metrics covered: Product (activation, retention, churn), Marketing (CAC, conversion, leads), Pricing (ARPU, LTV)
- **Pricing Strategies**: pricing strategies (Freemium, Subscription, One-time payment, Pay-per-use), factors affecting price
- **Growth Loops and Retention**: growth loops, retention strategies, revenue simulation with AI, Spanish market situation
- **Legal and Fiscal Aspects**: introduction to digital business legality in Spain, covering the key laws affecting digital businesses in Spain: **LSSI** (Ley de Servicios de la Sociedad de la Informacion), **LOPD/RGPD (GDPR - the European data protection regulation)**, and the **Ley de Consumidores** (Consumer Law). Includes the data minimization principle, user digital rights (Right to be Forgotten, explicit consent), the extraterritoriality principle in data protection, obligations to protect user data and notify security breaches, plus registering as autonomo vs forming an SL, invoicing, and basic accounting

Note on data protection coverage: the legal unit teaches the European data protection framework (LOPD/RGPD - GDPR) as it applies to digital businesses in Spain - what the law requires (user data protection, breach notification, consent, data minimization). It is a legal-awareness unit within Module 5, not a standalone web-development module.
